A LIGHT TO THE WORLD

MORE THAN A SUNDAY EXPERIENCE

Rooted in Scripture.
Growing together in Christ.

For generations, Bright Light Church has been a place where families gather to hear God's Word, pray together and shine His light in the world.

We are committed to biblical teaching, meaningful worship and genuine Christian fellowship. Whether you have followed Jesus for decades or are seeking answers, you are welcome to join us as we grow in faith.
