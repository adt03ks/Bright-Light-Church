COME TOGETHER. GROW TOGETHER.

Come for the movie. Stay for the sisterhood.

Life is richer when we don't walk through it alone. The Bright Light Women's Fellowship & Movie Club is a warm, welcoming space where women of all ages and walks of life can relax, be heard, laugh together, and form friendships that reach far beyond a single gathering.

Each month, we'll gather around a meaningful movie or shared reading, explore the lessons that speak to our lives, and encourage one another through honest conversation, prayer, and the Word of God. Whether you're seeking new friendships, a stronger walk with Christ, or simply a moment to be refreshed, you belong here.

✦ Rooted in the Spirit of Jesus Christ. Growing in faith, together.
