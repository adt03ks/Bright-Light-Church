Find and connect your existing church profile

Already listed in the church directory? Enter your details so church administrators can connect this login to your original membership record. Your records, household, anniversary, and prior history will stay together.

Privacy: The church will check for matching email, telephone number, or full name. Matching profiles are never shown to other members. An administrator verifies the correct person before linking an account.

Don't see your original profile? Submit this form anyway. Staff can create a new membership profile if no existing record belongs to you. For a minor, the church should confirm guardian authorization before linking.

Church administrators will verify and link spouse and household profiles. Your household's other profiles will not be accessible here.
