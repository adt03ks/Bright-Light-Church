Official Prize Drawing Rules & Eligibility
NO PURCHASE OR DONATION NECESSARY. Making a donation does not increase the chances of winning. Participation in the prize drawing is separate from all voluntary donations to Bright Light Church.

1. Organizer

This promotional prize drawing is organized by Bright Light Church, located at 513 FM 2041, Anahuac, Texas 77514.

2. Eligibility

The drawing is open to legal residents of Texas who are at least 18 years of age at the time of registration.

Individuals responsible for administering the drawings and members of their immediate households are not eligible. Void where prohibited by law.

3. Registration Period

Registration opens when the official drawing registration page is published.

Registration closes Saturday, November 14, 2026, at 11:59 PM Central Standard Time.

The drawings will take place on Sunday, November 15, 2026.

4. How to Enter

Eligible individuals may enter at no cost by completing the free prize drawing registration form on the official Bright Light Church fundraiser website.

Participants must provide their full name, valid telephone number, and email address.

Each eligible individual is limited to one free drawing registration. Duplicate registrations may be disqualified.

Each eligible registration will be included in the drawings for all five featured prizes. Participants do not select an individual prize.

Registration confirmation codes are used for recordkeeping and are not proof of winning or payment.

5. Voluntary Donations

Bright Light Church welcomes voluntary donations in support of its ministry and community efforts.

Donations may be submitted separately through the church's Givelify giving page.

Donations are not required to enter, participate, or claim a prize.

The amount donated has no effect on eligibility, number of entries, odds of winning, or prize selection.

6. Featured Prizes

Five prizes are featured in this event:

  • 65-inch VIZIO 4K QLED Smart Television
  • Movie Night Gift Basket featuring candy, coffee, snacks, and a ShowBiz Cinemas gift card
  • $100 Cactus Jack Restaurant Gift Card
  • Black and Gold Air Fryer
  • One-Night Staycation at Marriott Marquis Houston

Prize photographs are promotional representations. Final prize contents and applicable redemption conditions will be disclosed before the drawings.

The Marriott Marquis Houston staycation is subject to the actual reservation, certificate, or booking conditions provided with that prize.

7. Winner Selection

Winners will be selected by random drawing on November 15, 2026, from all eligible registrations.

Five prize winners will be selected, with one winner for each featured prize.

Each participant has an equal chance of selection. Odds depend on the total number of eligible entries.

An individual may receive no more than one prize during this event. Once selected as a winner, that individual will be removed from subsequent prize drawings.

8. Winner Notification

Winners will be contacted using the telephone number or email address supplied during registration.

Winners must respond within seven calendar days of notification to arrange prize acceptance.

If a winner cannot be contacted, is found ineligible, or declines a prize, the organizer may select an alternate winner at random.

9. Prize Acceptance

No donation or payment is required to claim a prize.

Winners may be required to provide reasonable proof of identity and eligibility.

Any applicable personal taxes are the responsibility of the winner.

Prize delivery, pickup arrangements, and redemption conditions will be communicated to the respective winners.

10. Fairness and Integrity

Bright Light Church will administer the drawings fairly and maintain records of eligible registrations, prize selections, and winner notifications.

Fraudulent registrations, duplicate entries, or attempts to manipulate the selection process may result in disqualification.

11. Privacy

Participant contact information will be used for administration of the drawing, eligibility verification, and winner notification.

Participant information will not be sold to outside marketing companies.

Any optional donation records will be managed separately for church financial reconciliation.

12. General Conditions

Participation is subject to applicable federal, state, and local laws.

Named prize manufacturers, restaurants, hotels, and payment providers are not sponsors or administrators of this drawing unless expressly identified as such by the organizer.

Bright Light Church reserves the right to address fraudulent activity, technical errors, and circumstances affecting the fair administration of the drawing, consistent with applicable law.
