WHO WE ARE

A history of God's faithfulness.

Bright Light Church began with a gathering of believers in 1937 and has continued to serve the Anahuac community through generations of worship, prayer and ministry.

Our Mission

To transform lives and reach generations of families by teaching, leading and living in the love God brings light to the world.

Our Core Values

Love. Come and be loved. Learn. Learn to love God and people. Live. Live set free. Light. Light up the world with God's love.
